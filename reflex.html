Your signal’s journey
  1. Eye: light hits your retina and becomes an electrical signal.
  2. Brain: recognizes “green!” and decides to move.
  3. Nerves: the command races down your spinal cord and arm at up to ~60 m/s (about 130 mph).
  4. Muscle: your finger taps.
